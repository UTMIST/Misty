# MLF funding policy

The Machine Learning Fellowship (MLF) funds a small number of member-led research
efforts each year. MLF is separate from CPSIF: a cost covered by one is not claimable
from the other, and the two have different eligibility rules.

## Eligibility requirements

An MLF application is considered when the applicant:

- Has been an active member for at least one full term before applying.
- Is not currently holding an open MLF award.
- Has a named faculty or alumni advisor who has confirmed in writing.
- Submits a scope that fits within two terms of part-time work.
- Has completed the research ethics acknowledgement for the current year.

An application missing the advisor confirmation is held rather than rejected, and the
applicant has four weeks to supply it before the application closes.

## Award amounts

A standard MLF award is $1,200 CAD disbursed in two instalments, the second released
after the midpoint report. A hardware-intensive project may request a supplement of up
to $800 CAD, which is reviewed separately and is not guaranteed by the main award.

## Year end

Unspent MLF budget at year end returns to the general departmental pool, on the same
schedule as CPSIF. An award that has released only its first instalment lapses if the
midpoint report has not arrived by the final reconciliation date.
