# 2025-2026 transition report

This report hands over the finance portfolio for the 2025-2026 year. It is shared with
the incoming finance lead only, and contains figures that have not been published.

## Departmental budget

The 2026-2027 departmental budget is $47,500 CAD, allocated across project funds,
events, and operations. CPSIF holds $12,000 of that total and MLF holds $9,600. The
remaining balance covers events and operating costs and is not committed to any fund.

## What carried over

Two reconciliations from the winter term closed late and are reflected in the opening
balance rather than the prior year's close. The AI² catering invoice was settled
after year end and appears in the current year.

## Recommendations

Move the reconciliation date two weeks earlier. The current date sits inside exam
period, which is the main reason claims arrive late and the main source of the late
reconciliations described above.
