# CUCAI 2027 logistics

The Canadian Undergraduate Conference on Artificial Intelligence (CUCAI) 2027 runs on
February 20-21, 2027 at the downtown conference centre. This document covers delegate
registration, the travel subsidy, and the booth schedule. Sponsorship tiers are not
covered here; see the AI² sponsorship package for tiers and benefits.

## Registration

The CUCAI delegate registration fee is $95 CAD for members and $140 CAD for
non-members. Registration opens on November 3, 2026 and closes once capacity is
reached, which in previous years happened before the posted deadline.

A delegate who registers and cannot attend may transfer their registration to another
member once, up to one week before the conference. Refunds are not issued after the
registration close date, and a transferred registration is not refundable by either
party.

## Travel subsidy

Delegates travelling more than 150 km may claim a CUCAI travel subsidy through CPSIF,
subject to the CPSIF travel limit rather than any separate CUCAI limit. The subsidy is
claimed after the conference using the ordinary CPSIF claim procedure.

## Booth schedule

Project booths are staffed in two shifts on each day, 10:00-13:00 and 13:00-16:00. A
project with fewer than two available members is paired with another project and shares
a booth. Booth assignments are published one week before the conference.
