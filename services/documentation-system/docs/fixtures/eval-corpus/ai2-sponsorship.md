# AI² sponsorship package

AI² is UTMIST's annual applied AI showcase. This package lists sponsorship tiers and
what each includes. It is distinct from GenAI Genesis and from the Academic Journal
Workshops, which run their own sponsorship arrangements.

## Tiers

- **Gold — $5,000 CAD.** Logo on the main stage backdrop, a staffed booth for both
  days, a speaking slot in the opening session, and access to the resume book.
- **Silver — $2,500 CAD.** Logo on printed signage, a staffed booth for one day, and
  access to the resume book.
- **Bronze — $1,000 CAD.** Logo on printed signage and acknowledgement in the closing
  session.

A sponsor may upgrade a tier up to four weeks before the event, paying the difference.
Downgrades are not offered once the signage order is placed.

## Resume book

The resume book is shared with Gold and Silver sponsors only, two weeks after the
event, and contains only the records of attendees who opted in.
