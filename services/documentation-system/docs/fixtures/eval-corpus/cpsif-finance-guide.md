# CPSIF funding guide

The Club Project and Student Initiative Fund (CPSIF) supports project teams that need
materials, compute, or event costs beyond their departmental allocation. This guide
covers eligibility, the claim procedure, and what happens to unspent funds.

## Eligibility

A project qualifies for CPSIF support when all of the following hold:

- The project has an active lead listed in the current departmental roster.
- At least two active members are attached to the project.
- The spending category appears in the approved category list below.
- No other UTMIST fund has already covered the same expense.

Projects that have carried an overdue reconciliation for more than one term are not
eligible until that reconciliation is closed, regardless of the categories involved.

## Approved categories

Hardware and components, cloud compute credits, event catering, printing and signage,
and travel to a presenting venue are all approved. Subscriptions longer than one term,
personal equipment, and honoraria are not approved and will be returned unpaid.

## Submitting a claim

Claims are submitted after the expense, not before. Pre-approval is a separate process
and does not substitute for a claim.

1. Collect the original itemized receipt. A bank or credit card statement is not an
   itemized receipt and will be returned.
2. Confirm the expense falls inside an approved category listed above. A claim in an
   unapproved category is rejected at intake and does not enter the review queue.
3. Record the project name exactly as it appears in the departmental roster. A claim
   whose project name does not resolve is held until the submitter corrects it.
4. Convert any non-CAD amount using the rate on the date of purchase, and attach the
   rate source alongside the receipt.
5. Submit the claim form to the finance lead before the term's reconciliation date.
   Claims arriving after that date are considered in the following term.
6. Keep the original receipt until the reimbursement has settled. Finance may request
   it a second time during reconciliation.

The reimbursement limit for a single CPSIF claim is $450 CAD. A larger expense needs an
exception approved before purchase, and an exception approved after purchase does not
make an over-limit claim payable.

## Travel

Travel to a presenting venue is reimbursable at actual cost up to $300 CAD per person
per event. Accommodation is reimbursable only when the venue is more than 150 km from
campus. Rideshare is reimbursable; parking is not.

## Unspent funds and reconciliation

Unspent CPSIF budget does not carry forward. At year end any remaining balance returns
to the general departmental pool and is redistributed during the following year's
allocation. A project expecting to underspend should notify the finance lead before the
final reconciliation date so the balance can be reallocated rather than returned.

## Contact

Questions about CPSIF eligibility or a held claim go to the finance lead at
finance@utmist.example. Questions about an approved category go to the same address.
